The Method

Every Ilmodo facial is assembled from these, chosen for your skin on the day — never a fixed protocol.

Disincrustationfollicular cleansing

Galvanic current run in reverse to saponify hardened sebum inside the follicle. It softens the plug before anything is extracted, so comedones lift out instead of being forced. This is the step that makes a deep follicular cleanse comfortable rather than traumatic.

Ultrasound Bladedecongesting

A vibrating steel spatula that uses sound waves and water to lift debris, softened sebum and dead cells out of the follicle. Unclogs and decongests with no grit and no scrubbing.

Chemical Peelsresurfacing

Acids that break the desmosomal bonds holding keratinocytes to one another, so dead cells release rather than being abraded off. Thins the stratum corneum, accelerates cell turnover, decongests follicles and opens the way for everything applied afterward. Lifts discoloration, evens tone, and at the right depth supports collagen synthesis while calming inflammation. Strength is chosen for your barrier that day, not from a fixed menu.

Direct High Frequencygermicidal

A glass electrode passed over the skin, producing ozone at the surface with a germicidal effect on bacteria. Used after extractions and over active breakouts — so that what has just been opened doesn't become the next thing you're treating.

Iontophoresisdermal hydration

A low-level galvanic current that drives water-soluble actives past the stratum corneum and into the dermis, where topical application alone will not reach. This corrects dermal dehydration rather than surface dryness — the difference between skin short of water and skin short of oil.

Ultrasoundsmoothing & contour

High-frequency mechanical vibration delivered into the tissue to temporarily plump and smooth the surface, minimising the appearance of fine lines and giving mild lift and contour definition. The effect is real and it is temporary, which is exactly why it's timed to the week of an event.

Microcurrenttone & lift

Low-level current that works on the muscle beneath the skin rather than the surface of it — re-educating tone, firming and lifting, and softening the look of fine lines as contour returns. Cumulative by nature: it builds across a course rather than arriving in a single sitting.

Lymphatic Drainagede-puffing

Slow, rhythmic manual work that moves stagnant lymph out of the face and neck. Clearing that congestion is the root of it; the increase in circulation and the better-oxygenated skin that follows are the visible effects. Puffiness settles, and the jaw and eyes get their definition back.
